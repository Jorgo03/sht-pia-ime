# Shtëpia.ime logo — "Dera" (the door)

An open door whose knob is the dot in **.ime**: welcome home.

## Colours
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neon orange | `#FF5F1F` | 255 95 31 | The door, always |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Light background |
| Black | `#000000` | 0 0 0 | Dark background |
| Ink | `#141414` | 20 20 20 | The name on light backgrounds |

CMYK ≈ 0 75 95 0 and Pantone ≈ Orange 021 C are approximations: approve a printed swatch first.
Contrast: orange on white 3.0:1, on black 6.9:1.

## Versions
- **Light:** orange door on white, name in ink (`lockup-horizontal-light.svg`, `lockup-stacked-light.svg`)
- **Dark:** orange door on black, name in white (`*-dark.svg`)
- **One colour:** all black (`symbol-black.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-black.svg`) or all white (`symbol-white.svg`) for stamps, embossing and single-colour print
- **Small sizes (32 px and under):** `symbol-small-black.svg`, with a heavier frame and a bigger knob; favicons use it

## Clear space and minimum size
- Keep empty space around the logo at least the **width of the door frame's post** (about 1/7 of the door's width) on every side.
- Minimum: door 16 px tall on screen (use the small-size cut), 8 mm tall in print.

## App icons
- iPhone: `app-icon-light.svg` (white) and `app-icon-dark.svg` (black, shown in Dark Mode on iOS 18+); `app-icon-tinted.svg` for tinted mode.
- Android: `android-foreground.svg` on a white background; `android-monochrome.svg` for themed icons. Android has no dark-mode icon.
- Web: `favicon.svg` switches white/black with the browser theme.

## Don't
- Don't change the orange, add gradients, shadows or outlines to the door.
- Don't put the orange door on mid-tone or busy photos; use the white or black version.
- Don't stretch, rotate or rearrange the door and the name.
- Don't fill the knob: it is a hole that shows the background.
