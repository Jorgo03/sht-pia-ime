What each status means here:

- ✅ **valid**: a real place of that name lies inside the outline of the city's own municipality (bashki).
- ✏️ **misspelled**: the place is real and in the right municipality, but spelled differently (proposed spelling given). "Probably" means one or two letters differ and it needs your confirmation.
- 🔁 **wrong hierarchy**: real, but in a different municipality from the city it is listed under (within 40 km). The proposal says which city or municipality it belongs to.
- 📍 **bad coordinates**: a point outside Albania, or outside its own municipality.
- ❌ **does not exist**: no trace in any source checked. The proposal is to remove it. No replacement is invented.
- ❓ **unverified**: something of that name exists, but not as a lagje or village (a street, a square, a beach, a lake, a market), or only other things are named after it. These need a decision or a local source (cadastre, the municipality) before keeping.

None of the zones affects user data: the three live listings have no zone set. Tiranë and Durrës, the cities with listings, are both valid.
